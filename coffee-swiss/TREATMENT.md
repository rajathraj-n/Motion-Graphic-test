# Seven Steps to a Cup: Swiss Motion Graphics

A ~36 s film, 1920×1080, 24 fps. English on-screen type, no voice-over: the layout's notes carry the words. Original synthesized score and foley.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A timetable.** A 24-row grid of the bean's year fills hour by hour. It's rigorous but slow, and the hours don't mean much for coffee.
2. **The finished cup dismantled.** We open on a black disc of 70 dots, then cut it apart back into the seed. It's a strong opening, but it reveals the ending first.
3. **A programme of seven steps with one exception (chosen).** Seven numbered pages on one long strip, each step a rule the system applies. One red dot (the bean) is the only thing not on the grid: it drifts in linearly, survives every rule, and becomes the full stop of the last word.

**Why 3:** the bean is a natural "exception" character, and the steps of a process suit "snap = beat". The ending is the bean finding its place in the type.

## 2. Logline and arc

One red dot travels through seven ruled pages, from seed to cup, and ends as the full stop in the word *coffee.*

- **Setup:** an empty page; the red dot drifts in and the grid grows from it.
- **Turn:** at "roast" everything stops (one silent bar), then a giant **200°** slams in. After the heat the system cuts things apart: the grind.
- **Ending:** a wall of all seven pages; 69 black dots ripple into a cup, and the red one lands last. Then every page is knife-cut away, and the dot is left as the period of "coffee."

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from** 1960s Zurich exhibition posters (grid, flush-left type, scale contrast) and modern identity-in-motion reels (snaps on the beat, wipes, no fades).
- **Don't take** any historic poster composition, concentric or fan layouts, Helvetica/Akzidenz, or a real institution's name.

## 5. Beat sheet (120 bpm, beat 0.5 s, bar 2 s)

- 0.0: paper. 0.0–1.5: the dot glides in linearly from the left edge and stops off-grid. 1.5–2.0: grid lines grow out from it.
- 2.0: "01" climbs in, "seed." 2.5: the soil bar wipes in under the dot. 3.0: note.
- 6.0: pan → 02 "three years." Bars rise on 6.5 / 7.0 / 7.5 (year 1, 2, 3); the dot rides the third.
- 10.0: pan → 03 "two seeds." The dot scales up to a cherry; 11.0 knife line; 11.5 the halves part.
- 14.0: pan → 04 "three weeks." 21 squares fill on 16ths, ordered by distance from the dot.
- 18.0: hard stop. Page 05 empty, only the dot.
- 19.0: **200°** thunks in. 19.5–21: heat bars; 21.0–22.0 rotate 90°, hold.
- 23.0: pan → 06 "grind." Push into the block; cuts on 24.0 / 25.0 / 26.0.
- 27.0: pull out to the wall. 28.0–30.0: 69 dots ripple in by distance. 30.5: the red dot lands last.
- 31.0: knife cuts sweep the wall, nearest first. 32.0: "coffee" clip-reveals, the dot slides in as its period. Hold to 36.0.

## 6. Cue map

- **Tempo:** 120 bpm, 18 bars.
- Bar 1: one sine tone following the dot (the exception sings).
- Bars 2–3: marimba ostinato. Each step adds an instrument: 02 kick, 03 bass, 04 hats, 05 (after the silence) full band.
- Bars 10–13: thinned to bass + marimba for the grind cuts.
- Bars 14–15: the full band.
- Bars 16–18: drums out, marimba and a final chord on 32.0.
- **Silence:** 18.0–19.0, a hard stop on the downbeat.
- **Hits:** every pan, numeral and cut lands on a beat.

## 8. Type

Archivo (OFL) at several weights, the only family. Numerals are 380 px bold, step words 120 px, notes 26 px medium with a 1 px rule above, technical corner notes 19 px. Everything is flush left on the 12-column grid. Text clip-reveals up from the baseline and leaves upward. No subtitles: there is no voice-over, and the notes are the words.

**Facts used:** an arabica plant bears its first fruit at about 3 years; a cherry usually holds 2 seeds; sun-drying takes about 3 weeks; roasting runs at about 200 °C; about 70 beans make one cup (common rule of thumb, ~7 g).
