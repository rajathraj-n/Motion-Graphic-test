# Specification for One Cup: Blueprint

A 40 s film, 1920×1080, 24 fps. English drawing notes on screen, no voice-over. Original synthesized score (harpsichord, bassoon, celesta) and foley.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A teardown in reverse.** A finished coffee machine is erased part by part down to one centre line. It's elegant, but it's about a machine, not the coffee.
2. **Revisions of one cup (REV. A–F).** Each revision is one step of processing. It's clever with the title block, but static: one figure redrawn.
3. **One sheet, four figures on one path of force (chosen).** A single centre line crosses the empty sheet; along it the drawing builds Fig. 1 *cherry*, Fig. 2 *drum roaster*, Fig. 3 *burr grinder*, Fig. 4 *cup*. A small bean outline travels the line between figures. The title block's STATUS field carries the verdict.

**Why 3:** coffee is a sequence of operations on one thing, and a centre line the bean travels along is a "track along a path of force".

## 2. Logline and arc

A coffee bean travels a white centre line across a cyanotype sheet, through a roaster and a grinder, until the drawing is stamped **BREWED** and a real cup leaves its ring on the title block.

- **Setup:** an empty blue sheet; one centre line crosses it on the first beat.
- **Turn:** the roaster's gauge needle climbs to 200 °C and overshoots (joy). Then a 2× cut to the gears meshing tooth by tooth: transformation.
- **Ending:** pull back to the whole sheet on the table. The red stamp lands (the only non-blue colour, once), then a coffee ring blooms on the title block, its tide line the last circle of the design.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from** 19th-century patent-drawing grammar (Fig. 1 / Fig. 2, numbered balloons, fine-line hatching, section lines) and animated technical end-title sequences (drafting order, live dimensions).
- **Don't take** any real patent, real machine, brand, signature, or the library demo's cloud machine, rain or revision cloud.

## 5. Beat sheet (96 bpm, beat 0.625 s, bar 2.5 s)

- 0.0–1.25: the centre line draws across the empty sheet.
- 1.25–6.0: Fig. 1 in drafting order: centre lines, outlines (skin, pulp, two beans), crease detail, pulp hatching, Ø dimension, balloons 1–3, label. NOTE 1 at 4.4.
- 7.5–8.75: the bean outline travels the line to Fig. 2.
- 8.75–10.6: drum, vanes, hopper, burner, gauge draw in. 10.6–14.4: the drum steps one vane per 8th; flames flicker on beats; the needle climbs 0 → 200, overshoots, settles. Live label "T = n °C". NOTE 2 at 11.25.
- 15.0: the bean travels on. 15.6–17.5: grinder gears and hopper draw in.
- 17.5: CUT 2× to the mesh. Gears advance one tooth per 8th; grounds (hatch dots) fall. NOTE 3 at 17.8.
- 20.0: cut back; 20.6–21.9 track to Fig. 4.
- 22.0–24.4: cup outline, saucer, handle. 24.4–26.25: the section line sweeps; liquid hatching fills to the level. 25.6: dimensions "250 ml", "≈ 70 beans". NOTE 4 at 25.0. Steam as hidden (dashed) lines.
- 28.75–30: hold, near silence.
- 30.0–31.25: pull out to the whole sheet.
- 31.25: title block fields letter in. 32.5: **STAMP** (red), thump, cadence.
- 34.4: coffee ring blooms on the title block. Hold to 40.

## 6. Cue map

- 96 bpm, 16 bars.
- **Bars 1–3:** harpsichord two-voice invention in G minor, a motive per figure.
- **Bars 4–6:** bassoon joins (the roaster's line). Harpsichord on 8ths = the drum's escapement.
- **Bars 7–8:** grinder: harpsichord canon at the octave; celesta ticks on each gear tooth.
- **Bars 9–11:** full ensemble, rising.
- **28.75–30:** written-out ritardando into near silence (paper sounds only).
- **30–32.5:** suspended chord.
- **32.5:** full cadence on the stamp.
- **34.4:** one celesta note for the ring. Tail.

## 8. Type

- Architects Daughter (hand lettering) for the header, labels, notes and note boxes.
- B612 for title-block fields.
- Notes are lettered on left to right with a glowing nib, in a translucent box with double white rules and a "NOTE / n" cell, placed in empty sky per shot.
- Each note is also lettered into the sheet's GENERAL NOTES column.

**Facts used:** a cherry holds 2 seeds; drum roasting at about 200 °C for about 12 min; burr grinders crush beans between two burrs; about 70 beans (~7 g) per cup; a cup here is 250 ml.
