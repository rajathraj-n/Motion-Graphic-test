# Where a Cup Begins: Watercolor Brush

A ~40 s film, 1920×1080, 24 fps. English on-screen hand notes, no voice-over. Original synthesized score (kalimba, felt piano, soft pad) and foley.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A year in one locked journal page.** The plant flowers, fruits and is harvested on one view. It's calm, but roasting and the cup never fit on one plant.
2. **The cup painted backwards.** We open on a finished cup and unpaint it back to the seed. It's clever, but reads as an effect rather than a journey.
3. **One tall journal sheet, read top to bottom (chosen).** A hand-written question at the top, then the answer painted down the page, entry by entry: branch, cherry, cross-section, drying, roast swatches, cup. The camera tilts down like a reader's eye. At the end it pulls back to show the whole column at once.

**Why 3:** a process reads naturally down a page, and the vertical tilt is this medium's own move. The pull-back makes the journey into one painted object.

## 2. Logline and arc

A question written on blank paper, *"Where does a cup of coffee begin?"*, is answered by painting, from a flowering branch to a steaming cup.

- **Setup:** the question, handwritten.
- **Turn:** the cherry is cut open and two pale seeds appear: the beans were inside a fruit all along. The roast swatches then move green → brown.
- **Ending:** coffee is poured, steam rises, and the camera pulls back to the whole sheet. The last note answers the first: *"It begins as a seed, about 3 years before."*

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from** naturalist field-journal plates (specimen + handwritten note, generous margins) and botanical-illustration timelapses (structure before detail).
- **Don't take** any specific botanical plate's composition, or the library demo's map, dolly or rain.

## 5. Beat sheet (84 bpm, beat 0.714 s)

- 0.3–2.8: the question writes on, left to right.
- 4.0: tilt starts. 4.5–7.5: branch, then leaves, paint in stroke by stroke. 7.0: white flowers. 8.5–10.5: cherries paint green and ripen to red (time change). 9.5: note "Coffea arabica — first fruit after about 3 years".
- 12.0: push in to the cut cherry (specimen). 13–15.5: skin, pulp, two seeds paint in. 15.0: leader notes "skin", "pulp", "2 seeds = 2 beans".
- 19.0: tilt on. 19.5–21.5: drying bed of pale beans; note "sun-dried · about 3 weeks".
- 22.0–25.0: four roast swatches paint left to right (green → cinnamon → brown → dark); mono labels; note "roasted at about 200 °C".
- 27.0: tilt to the cup. 27.5–29.5: cup paints in. 29.5–31.5: coffee pours (a dark wash fills). 31.5: steam strokes. 32.0: note "about 70 beans in one cup".
- 34.0–36.5: pull-back to the whole sheet. 36.5: the answer note writes on. Hold to 40.0.

## 6. Cue map

- **Tempo:** 84 bpm.
- **0–4:** pencil and room only (silence of the brush), then a kalimba motif enters on 3.6.
- **4–12:** kalimba arpeggio, soft pad (C major add9 → A minor).
- **12–19:** thinned to kalimba alone, slower notes: leaning in.
- **19–27:** felt piano joins, with a gentle rising line through the roast swatches.
- **27–34:** full (kalimba, felt piano, pad). The pour is a J-cut: its sound starts 0.3 s before the picture.
- **33.3–34.0:** near silence (brush lifted).
- **34–40:** a pull-back chord swell, ending on one kalimba note at 36.5 under the answer.

## 7. Sound design

| Section | Bed | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | quiet room | pencil scratch | none → kalimba |
| Branch | birds, light breeze | brush rustles per stroke group | kalimba + pad |
| Cherry | room | knife-soft brush, pencil notes | kalimba alone |
| Drying / roast | warm air, a faint roast crackle | rustles, swatch dabs | + felt piano |
| Cup | room | pour, cup clink, steam hush | full |
| Pull-back | room | page rustle | swell, last note |

## 8. Type

- Caveat (hand) for notes, drawn over a paper-coloured halo and revealed left to right, with pen leader lines.
- Cormorant Garamond Italic for the question and the answer.
- IBM Plex Mono, widely tracked, for measured labels (swatch minutes, °C).
- No subtitles: no voice-over.

**Facts used:** an arabica plant first fruits at about 3 years; a cherry has skin, pulp and usually 2 seeds; sun-drying takes about 3 weeks; roasting at about 200 °C for about 10–14 min; about 70 beans make one cup (common rule of thumb).
